# Third party assets

What ships in this repository and where it comes from.

## Music and sound

The ten jukebox tracks, the pinball arcade bed, and the brick, dart, gun and explosion
samples are royalty free. No attribution is required and none is claimed as original
composition.

## Font

**Uncial Antiqua** by Astigmatic (Brian J. Bonislawsky), used for the jukebox track name.
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, version 1.1.
The license text is at https://openfontlicense.org and the font ships in
`public/fonts/uncial-antiqua.woff2`.

## KTX2 transcoder

**Basis Universal** transcoder, `public/basis/basis_transcoder.js` and
`basis_transcoder.wasm`, loaded by three.js to decode the KTX2 textures.
Copyright 2019 Binomial LLC. Licensed under Apache License 2.0.
The license text is at http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

## Models and textures

Every model and texture in `public/` is authored for this project by its Blender scripts
in `art/`. The source images those scripts bake from live in `art/source/`.
